Privacy policy — personal projects

These applications access only the operator's own Google account data, on the operator's own computers, for the operator's own use. No data is collected from other people, and no data is shared with or sold to anyone. There are no users other than the operator.

If you somehow authorized one of these applications with your own Google account, revoke it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
